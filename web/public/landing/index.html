Features

Everything an Afghan company needs to manage its workforce — in one app

GPS Attendance

Clock in and out with location. Geofencing ensures the employee is at the work site.

Face Recognition

Attend with your face — no fingerprint reader or card needed. Prevents buddy punching.

Leave Management

Apply, approve and reject leave — with balance tracking and approval chains.

Automated Payroll

Monthly salary, daily wage and piece-rate — tax, deductions and payslips calculated automatically.

Enterprise Security

Every company fully isolated. Roles and permissions — manager, team lead, employee.

Trilingual

Dari, Pashto and English. Solar Hijri calendar with Afghan month names. AFN currency.
